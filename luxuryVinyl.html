WHAT IS LVP?
Luxury Vinyl Plank —
Built Different

Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) is a multi-layer product that goes far beyond traditional sheet vinyl. It's semi-rigid, nearly five times thicker than standard sheet vinyl, and snaps together without glue — making it one of the easiest floors to install.

Each plank is built with a scratch-resistant aluminum oxide top coat, a clear protective film, a photo-realistic design layer, and a strong rigid vinyl core — all working together to deliver beauty that holds up to real life.

INSTALLATION
Easy Click-Lock Installation

One of the biggest advantages of luxury vinyl plank is how it goes in. Using a click-lock system, the edges and ends of each plank snap together cleanly — no glue, no mess. The result is a floating floor that's installed faster and can be replaced section by section if ever needed.
