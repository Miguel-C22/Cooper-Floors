WHAT IS LAMINATE?
A Smart, Synthetic
Hard Surface Floor

Laminate is a synthetic composite product with a moisture-resistant stabilizing base layer topped with synthetic fiberboards that provide volume and strength. On top sits a photographic layer that gives the plank its design and color, along with a clear resin layer for added durability.

Laminate is a popular choice for living areas, kitchens, dining rooms, bedrooms, and hallways — anywhere you want the beauty of hardwood without the higher price tag.

INSTALLATION
Tongue-and-Groove
Installation

Laminate planks are joined from one piece to the next using a tongue-and-groove system, forming a single heavy unit that won't shift or slide. Depending on the type, planks are either snapped together or glued.

A foam or felt underlayment sits between the subfloor and laminate, providing a softer footfall and detaching the two surfaces. The result is a stable, floating floor that's comfortable underfoot and built to last.
