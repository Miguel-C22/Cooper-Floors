A Family Business Built on Lasting Value

What started in our home has grown into a trusted flooring business, built on hard work, family, and a commitment to doing things the right way.

OUR STORY
A Journey That
Started at Home

Both hailing from a rural farm town in South Dakota, Gary and Jan's journey together began when they tied the knot and later set their sights on Minnesota in 1962.

Upon their arrival, Gary swiftly secured a position as a floor installer. Over time, he transitioned into the world of carpet buying and selling, starting from their home and eventually establishing a storefront in Spring Lake Park.

What started as a shared vision has grown into Cooper's Floors — a business built on quality products, honest service, and lasting relationships with our customers.
