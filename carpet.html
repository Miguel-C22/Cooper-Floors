Nylon

Polyester

CARPET STYLES & TEXTURES
Styles & Textures for Your Home

Not only does carpet come in a variety of materials, but it also is composed of various pile heights, patterns, and textures to deliver optimal performance and beautiful designs. Depending on the room and your lifestyle, you can choose from the following carpet styles:

Loop
Yarn loops are left uncut, creating a durable, textured surface great for high-traffic areas.
Cut
Loops are cut for a soft, plush finish — perfect for bedrooms and living rooms.
Cut-Loop
A combination of cut and looped fibers that creates visual patterns and added texture.
